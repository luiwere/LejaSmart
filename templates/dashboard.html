Why Move Away From Paper?

The Difference Between Lost Profits and Business Growth

See how LejaSmart transforms messy, stressful bookkeeping into an effortless daily habit.

❌

The Traditional Paper Ledger ("Kitabu")

  • Lost & Forgotten Expenses: Daily cash spent on casual labor, transport, and lunches slips away unrecorded.
  • Stockouts & Spoilage: Fast-moving items run out without notice, while expiring stock goes unnoticed on high shelves.
  • Zero Remote Visibility: When you're away from the shop, you have no idea how much money was collected.
  • Exhausting Month-End Calculations: Spending entire weekends adding up receipts with a handheld calculator.
  • Accountant Disconnect: Accountants receive incomplete notes, causing delayed tax filings and inaccurate books.
Smart Choice
✅

The Modern LejaSmart Solution

  • Voice-to-Text Expense Logging: Speak your expenses in seconds. Every shilling is categorized with notes and supplier names.
  • Automated Reorder & Expiry Alerts: Know exactly when stock is running low and when perishable products near expiration.
  • Real-Time Remote Dashboard: Check live shop revenue, expenses, and net profit from your phone wherever you are.
  • Instant 1-Second P&L Statements: Filter by day, week, or custom dates to see pure gross profit and net earnings immediately.
  • Secure Shop Code Sync: Share a 6-character Shop Code with your accountant for instant, read-only reconciliation.
